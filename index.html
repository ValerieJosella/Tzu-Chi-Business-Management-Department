Introduction系所簡介

Business education with a human heart以人文為本的商管教育

The Department of Business Management at Tzu Chi University in Hualien teaches the full range of business disciplines, from marketing and operations to finance and human resources, with a focus on social innovation and sustainable management.慈濟大學經營管理學系位於花蓮，課程涵蓋行銷、營運、財務與人力資源等商管領域，並重視社會創新與永續經營理念。

Students learn in small classes, work on real projects with industry, and can join internships and study programs abroad.學生在小班環境中學習，與業界合作實作專題，並有機會參與海內外實習與交流。

Our history系所沿革

  1. 1989
    Roots in Hualien花蓮創校

    The Tzu Chi College of Nursing is founded in Hualien. It later grows into Tzu Chi University of Science and Technology.慈濟護理專科學校於花蓮創立，日後發展為慈濟科技大學。

  2. 2015
    A university of science and technology改名科技大學

    The school is renamed Tzu Chi University of Science and Technology (TCUST).學校改名為「慈濟科技大學」。

  3. TCUST慈科大
    Marketing and Distribution Management行銷與流通管理系

    At TCUST, the department trains students in marketing, distribution and business operations.在慈濟科技大學時期，本系培育行銷、流通與經營管理人才。

  4. 2024
    One Tzu Chi University兩校合併

    In August 2024, TCUST merges into Tzu Chi University, and the department becomes the Department of Business Management.2024年8月，慈濟科技大學併入慈濟大學，本系更名為經營管理學系。
